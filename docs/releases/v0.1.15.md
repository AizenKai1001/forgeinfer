## Forge Studio and ForgeInfer v0.1.15 (pre-release)

**Drop a screenshot or a photo of a page on the window.** Pictures (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.bmp`, `.gif`, `.tif`, `.webp`) are read like a scanned PDF page: on Windows the text recognition that comes with the system reads the text in them, and the chat can be asked about it (a screenshot of an error message, a photo of notes or of a printed page). Nothing is downloaded and nothing leaves the computer; on macOS and Linux the message says that this is built into Windows only.

Tried on two pictures drawn for the test (a compiler error as a PNG and as a JPEG: all three lines came out, the one word in back quotes after the others because the recognition orders text blocks by itself) and on the pages of a real scan; photographs taken at an angle, handwriting and small print will be worse.

Also: the Chat with documents section of the Studio guide is rewritten as a table of what can be attached and short paragraphs for short files, long files, names and codes, summaries and what you see, instead of one long paragraph.

Everything else is as in [v0.1.14](https://github.com/AizenKai1001/forgeinfer/releases/tag/v0.1.14).

Unsigned binaries. Studio has been run on Windows 11 with an RTX 4060 and on Ubuntu 24.04 (X11) with an RTX 2060 SUPER; the macOS build is compiled and unit-tested by CI but nobody has run its window yet.
