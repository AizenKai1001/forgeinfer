## Forge Studio and ForgeInfer v0.1.13 (pre-release)

**Names and identifiers are found by their words.** Searching a long file by meaning is weak where a question names something rare: for "what happened with invoice INV-7731-QX?" or "what did Marguerite Okafor pay for?" the vector of the right passage scores only 0.5 to 0.6 against the question, below the minimum score of 0.6, and for an identifier it can rank far down the list (74th of 120 in one test). Now the chat also looks for the words of the question that few passages have (in at most a thirtieth of the passages, twelve at most) with BM25, and the best passage that has them is cited whatever its vector says; the passage list marks it "has a word of the question". When the vectors already fill the answer a keyword match takes one place of the three, not more.

Measured with nomic-embed-text on a synthetic ledger of 120 passages with 30 questions (10 facts, each asked by its identifier, by the person, and in other words): the right passage was cited 17 times out of 20 with vectors alone and as a strong match 7 times; with the keyword search 20 and 20. The 10 questions in other words were 10 of 10 before and after, and four questions with no rare word got the same citations as before. The test is `scratch/retrieval_ids_test.py` in the private repository and a synthetic one: a real file with real questions may differ.

Also: **Settings > About has a "Check for updates" button**, which asks github.com for the newest release (one request, only when you press it). A file that makes a reader panic is now an error on screen instead of a "Reading ..." message that never ends. Embedding requests carry 16 passages instead of 32, so that Stop is felt sooner on the processor.

Everything else is as in [v0.1.12](https://github.com/AizenKai1001/forgeinfer/releases/tag/v0.1.12).

Unsigned binaries. Studio has been run on Windows 11 with an RTX 4060 and on Ubuntu 24.04 (X11) with an RTX 2060 SUPER; the macOS build is compiled and unit-tested by CI but nobody has run its window yet.
