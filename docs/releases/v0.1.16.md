## Forge Studio and ForgeInfer v0.1.16 (pre-release)

**PowerPoint: the text of SmartArt diagrams and the speaker notes are read.** A slide whose text is a SmartArt diagram (lists, processes, cycles made with the Insert > SmartArt button) kept that text in a part of its own, and Studio read only the slide's own file: such a slide came out short or empty. Now the diagram's text follows the slide's, and the speaker notes come under a "Notes:" line.

How it was found, and what else it showed: the readers were checked against Microsoft Office's own output on real documents. PowerPoint saved a real 6-slide deck as OpenDocument and the two readings were compared word by word: the .pptx reading lacked a whole reference list that was in a diagram (302 words against 380), and is equal after the change (380 against 380). The same comparison found nothing else: five real Word documents read through `.docx` and through the RTF that Word saved from them gave identical words; one of them also through `.odt`; a real Excel workbook read through `.xlsx` and through the `.ods` that Excel saved gave identical words. So the OpenDocument and RTF readers are no longer tested on synthetic files only.

Everything else is as in [v0.1.15](https://github.com/AizenKai1001/forgeinfer/releases/tag/v0.1.15).

Unsigned binaries. Studio has been run on Windows 11 with an RTX 4060 and on Ubuntu 24.04 (X11) with an RTX 2060 SUPER; the macOS build is compiled and unit-tested by CI but nobody has run its window yet.
