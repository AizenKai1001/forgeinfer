## Forge Studio and ForgeInfer v0.1.16 (pre-release)

**Fixes found by checking the document readers against Microsoft Office's own output.** Office was used as an oracle: Word, Excel and PowerPoint saved real documents (copies of school work: a memo, exercises, an article summary, a workbook, a 6-slide deck) in another format, and the text that Studio read from the original and from the copy was compared word by word. Three real faults came out, and each is fixed:

- **PowerPoint:** the text of SmartArt diagrams (lists, processes and cycles made with Insert > SmartArt) lives in a part of its own and was not read, so a slide made of a diagram came out short or empty (a reference list of 78 words was missing: 302 words against 380 for PowerPoint's own `.odp` copy; now 380 against 380). The speaker notes are read too, under a "Notes:" line.
- **Word:** footnotes and endnotes were not read. They now follow the text under "Footnotes:" and "Endnotes:". Headers, footers and comments are still left out.
- **Saved web pages:** a page saved by Word (and by other Office programs) is encoded as Windows-1252, and its curly quotes, dashes and accents came out as replacement characters, because the reader took every page for UTF-8. Pages are now decoded as UTF-8, UTF-16 or Windows-1252 (the encoding the page declares, or the one that fits its bytes); other legacy encodings (Shift-JIS, GBK, KOI8-R) are still not known.

What agreed from the start: five real Word documents read from `.docx` and from the RTF that Word saved gave identical words, one of them also from `.odt`, and a real Excel workbook read from `.xlsx` and from the `.ods` that Excel saved: so the OpenDocument and RTF readers, which until now had only been tried on files built by the tests, hold up on real Office output (LibreOffice's own files have not been tried).

Everything else is as in [v0.1.15](https://github.com/AizenKai1001/forgeinfer/releases/tag/v0.1.15).

Unsigned binaries. Studio has been run on Windows 11 with an RTX 4060 and on Ubuntu 24.04 (X11) with an RTX 2060 SUPER; the macOS build is compiled and unit-tested by CI but nobody has run its window yet.
