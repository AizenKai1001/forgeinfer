## Forge Studio and ForgeInfer v0.1.18 (pre-release)

**Word comments are read.** The comments of a `.docx` (a teacher's remarks, a reviewer's notes) now follow the footnotes and endnotes under a "Comments:" line, each after the name of its author, so that "what did the teacher comment?" has an answer. Checked on a document that Word itself made with a comment in it.

**Long spreadsheets say what their columns are, again and again.** In an `.xlsx` or `.ods` sheet of more than 40 rows, the sheet's name and its header row (the widest of the first rows, which is not the title row above it) are repeated under "(continued)" after every 30 rows. A passage cut out of the middle of a sheet used to be a bare list of values; now it says "Sheet: Stock (continued)" and the names of the columns first. Short sheets are unchanged. Checked on a real 92-row sheet (two repeats, nothing else changed) and by the unit tests.

Smaller: the part summaries of a long file are asked to leave out legal notices and page headers (this was in v0.1.17).

Everything else is as in [v0.1.17](https://github.com/AizenKai1001/forgeinfer/releases/tag/v0.1.17).

Unsigned binaries. Studio has been run on Windows 11 with an RTX 4060 and on Ubuntu 24.04 (X11) with an RTX 2060 SUPER; the macOS build is compiled and unit-tested by CI but nobody has run its window yet.
